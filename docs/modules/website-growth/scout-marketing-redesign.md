# Scout marketing specialist

Evidence status: implemented on the redesign branch; production activation requires the reviewed merge, runtime update, and owner configuration. On 2026-09-21 the owner authorized an initial editable operating profile based on the existing Newl business brief and pilot mission.

## Ownership and architecture

Newl Apps owns the marketing objective, priorities, qualified-enquiry definition, pilot success guidance, named competitor watchlist, rolling research budget, work history, approvals, and measurement evidence. The external Scout worker selects one due item, investigates it with public web search and a bounded context packet, then saves its result through the tenant-bound `/api/website-growth/scout/work-items` API. It cannot send, approve, publish, change priorities, or change permissions.

The initial mission allocates attention approximately 60% to warehousing, 30% to ocean/air freight and 10% to trucking, while preferring quality over quota. Charlotte/Southeast warehousing and fulfillment leads; Mississauga/GTA and Southern Ontario focus on case-pick, pallet, B2B replenishment, cross-border support and local trucking. Ocean/air focuses on smaller and midsized importers on established China–US/Canada and UK/Netherlands–Canada lanes, with Latin America treated as an evidence-led experiment. Account-specific capacity and high-touch D2C remain facts to verify, not promises.

A qualified enquiry is a real, identifiable business contact with a plausible current or planned need for a service and geography/lane Newl can serve, plus enough operating detail or timing to justify a discovery call or quote. Spam, students, vendor solicitations and direct competitors are excluded. A person must still confirm service fit before the inbound record is marked Qualified; Quote sent and Won remain separate outcomes. Scout must not invent a minimum volume or urgency.

The mission stores numerical pilot guidance as editable prose rather than executable gates. A strong business win is at least one newly human-qualified enquiry, Quote sent or Won outcome linked to the page. A useful page-level win is two additional enquiries or two material leading indicators: clicks/sessions increase by both 10 and 25% from a baseline of at least 20, CTR increases one percentage point with at least 100 impressions, or average position improves three places with at least 100 impressions. Low volume and unavailable evidence are not failures. Scout considers stopping/replacing a hypothesis only after two complete windows with no meaningful improvement or a material decline. This guidance shapes model judgment; deterministic evidence collection and human outcome labels remain authoritative.

The initial watchlist is Bonded Logistics and Piedmont Distribution Centers for Charlotte, Grey Wolf 3PL and G&S Direct for Mississauga warehousing/logistics, and Access Air and Setara Logistics for GTA forwarding/logistics. The mission records each domain and comparison reason. Scout must validate current public evidence for the chosen task; the list does not assert identical capabilities or create a continuous crawl.

The default `/website-growth` view is the marketing workboard. `/website-growth/pages` retains the complete page brief/build workspace, `/website-growth/backlinks` retains publisher approval/execution, and `/website-growth/signals` retains raw evidence. `/website-growth/marketing` is a stable direct workboard link.

One wake handles one research step. The model chooses the item and explains its selection; there is no prescribed sequence of marketing tactics. The application enforces 1–20 steps per rolling 24 hours and 1–10 items concurrently being researched or awaiting review. Defaults are six steps and three active items, with research paused. These are proposed operating defaults, not production activation or approved business targets.

## Durable work

Typed version-1 records reuse `AutomationJobRun`: `WEBSITE_GROWTH_SCOUT_MISSION`, `WEBSITE_GROWTH_SCOUT_WORK`, `WEBSITE_GROWTH_SCOUT_WAKE`, and `WEBSITE_GROWTH_SCOUT_STEP`. No database migration is needed. A wake record reports the actual scheduler check and its idle/run gate without consuming research budget; a step record starts with the claim and finishes with the work result. Tenant-derived deterministic IDs prevent duplicate reconciliation records; source records and every read/mutation retain `tenantId`. Serializable transactions enforce budgets and ownership, and revision comparisons reject concurrent/stale edits. Each claim gets a 30-minute lease. Abandoned running step records are expired after that lease instead of permanently looking active. The worker supplies an idempotency key and retries transient claim failures with that same key, so a committed claim whose response was lost returns its existing lease and step instead of creating more work. A lost completion acknowledgement is also idempotent for its lease. Unknown API failures log only their action, error class and provider error code; response bodies continue to hide private integration details.

Work states are Ready, Working, Needs review, Waiting, Done, and Dismissed. Waiting includes a dated next action. Build/uncertain-send waits are excluded from research claims. Owner feedback uses an explicit decision field, preserving the choice even when submission omits the clicked button. Recoverable review errors remain beside the form, and entered feedback survives a retry. The bounded event history retains recent decisions; immutable step jobs retain the rolling budget independently of history trimming.

Reconciliation finds existing page candidates, unanswered publisher replies, published page drafts, one replenishing open-ended research item, and one reusable whole-site review when saved site evidence exists. An owner can also add a named page investigation. A completed open-ended research brief makes room for the next one without a weekly quota; the whole-site record is reused when its chosen review date arrives. Dated waits and unresolved reviews remain respected. The worker carries recent completed and dismissed decisions into its next investigation, excluding private publisher conversations from public-search turns. A rejected/deferred research item is remembered instead of being selected repeatedly. New research or an outcome review can propose a new page: Scout then receives a page task and prepares the full brief before seeking owner publishing approval. Researched publisher prospects go through the existing relevance, quality, spam, dedupe, retention, and human-approval controls. No prospect promotion authorizes outreach.

Each scheduled wake first refreshes eligible saved evidence and reconciles those sources. It then records whether the mission was paused, no work was due, budget was exhausted, or active capacity was full. If any gate blocks work, the wrapper exits before supervisor selection, claim, or specialist investigation. That empty wake does not create a step-budget record. If work is due, the supervisor chooses one item; one wake never drains the entire queue. The external scheduler owns wake timing, while Newl Apps owns eligibility, claims, durable state, and approval boundaries.

Page results use the existing complete draft contract and claims/approval/build path. Every workboard read and claim projects the current tenant-scoped draft/build records; it does not wait for the hourly worker to notice an approval. Reconciliation persists the same projection. Approved drafts stop consuming research/review capacity. Running builds remain external waits, while failed/stale/missing builds and PR/preview review have explicit owner actions through the complete brief. Stale workboard forms cannot revise an approved draft. Published pages receive a separate outcome task. A later outcome review can open a fresh improvement for the same published page; equivalent active page work is reused. Raw analytics can still be refreshed independently through the existing weekday check-in.

## Supervision and the workboard

The existing worker now has three bounded turns: supervisor selection/direction (180 seconds), specialist investigation (900 seconds), and a separate quality review for a deliverable (180 seconds). The research lease starts after selection; these budgets plus bounded API calls fit the 30-minute lease. This is one resumable worker, not a network of independently scheduled agents. The supervisor receives the owner's direction, dated results, recent decisions, and cached competitive signals. A page investigation also receives the latest matched Search Console query/page evidence for the current and previous 28-day periods, together with recorded Scout publication dates. Repeated refresh rows for the same day are deduplicated and partial or missing periods remain explicit. Due measurements, revisions, replies, and exploratory research stay ahead of raw page candidates in the bounded selection packet. Same-route candidates are grouped and an active brief/build suppresses another page candidate for that route.

The review returns PASS, REVISE, or WAIT with a reason. Every WAIT records a blocker type, the exact missing evidence, the proposed resolution, and whether Scout can resolve it. Data refreshes, public research, low volume, external systems, and recoverable technical failures stay with Scout; only an irreducible private fact or business decision is classified as owner input. Newl Apps requires a valid PASS before creating a deliverable or promoting a proposal. Missing, failed, and legacy unchecked reviews preserve the artifact in a dated wait. Three incomplete quality/measurement attempts surface an owner support request and suspend automatic retries until the owner supplies direction. An ordinary low-volume measurement wait remains scheduled. A successful quality review conveys no approval to build, send, or publish. It is a model judgement, not a guarantee of correctness.

The workboard shows an “If Scout woke now” explanation, current rolling-budget/active-slot usage, and live counts for each work source. It separates Your decisions, Working now, Scheduled reviews, External systems, and a collapsed Due for Scout queue. “Due” means a ready item or arrived review date that needs no owner/external action, has no active same-route conflict, and fits the bounded one-per-route worker selection packet. It does not mean the worker has budget or active-slot capacity; “If Scout woke now” reports those separate run gates. Future evidence dates no longer share a generic queue with callbacks/builds, and due waits return to Due for Scout. Waiting cards show the structured blocker and whether Scout or an owner owns the next action. Only genuine owner actions show a decision form; due candidates are research options rather than an owner to-do list. The manual reconciliation button only checks saved records and does not run the AI worker or consume a research step. The board refreshes every 30 seconds while visible and on window focus, except while a form has focus, so it can reflect build callbacks without disturbing edits.

## Publisher conversations

Reply sync covers contacted and replied opportunities with recent outreach history, ignores already-recorded replies, prioritizes opt-outs, and continues tracking new replies after an owner-approved response. Scout prepares an exact subject and body. An Admin/Manager with mutation access sees the recipient and text and explicitly approves sending.

The server refreshes replies before sending, rechecks the recorded latest reply, original opportunity approval, consent evidence, country, suppression, and the shared daily follow-up/response cap. It stores human approval and reserves a unique message ID before calling Microsoft Graph. It adds the standard business/legal footer. Microsoft Graph acceptance is recorded as acceptance, not proof of delivery or placement. An uncertain result remains held for mailbox reconciliation and cannot be restarted through ordinary work review. This worker has no sending tool; sending occurs only through the authenticated human action.

## Outcome measurement

The baseline is the 28 complete days before publication. The initial comparison is days 1–28 after publication, excluding publication day and allowing four days for data availability. A deferred review uses a later 28-day window ending three days before the current date, retaining the original baseline. Search Console clicks/impressions, derived CTR and impression-weighted position, GA4 sessions/engaged sessions and engagement rate, and aggregate first-party website-form enquiries are fetched independently for the exact route. A failed source is unavailable, an empty analytics result is no matching rows, and neither becomes an invented zero. Successful empty first-party enquiry counts are zero. Manually entered inbound opportunities are excluded through the WEBSITE_FORM entry-method filter. Differences require both periods; percentage change from a zero baseline is null.

The server saves aggregate measurements separately from the model's interpretation and keeps up to five previous snapshots. The outcome task retains the original opportunity hypothesis. Scout recommends KEEP, ITERATE, STOP, or WAIT with confidence and limitations. Partial evidence can support a next experiment after quality review, but its interpretation is always labelled LOW confidence. No usable post-change evidence means a dated wait and eventual support escalation. One unavailable integration does not veto all progress. A reviewed informational outcome is recorded without requiring an owner approval. Recorded results and bounded research findings feed subsequent supervisor selection and investigation, including unsuccessful and deferred outcomes.

Scout must report evidence limitations, insufficient volume, seasonality, and concurrent changes. Before/after movement is association, not causal lift. Form submissions are enquiries; they are not qualified until a person applies the mission definition and records that status. Enquiries are grouped by submitted page URL, not attributed to individual GA4 sessions; this does not establish a conversion rate or revenue attribution. Completed briefs/builds are activity, not success metrics. The mission's numerical guidance is advisory and does not override evidence quality or create an automatic pass/fail rule.

Existing Semrush scheduled-report ingestion and the independent analytics check-in remain in place. The supervisor now reads tenant-scoped cached reports/tracking directly. Each report keeps its own observation date and eight-day freshness flag; a recent position report does not refresh an old competitor report. Report-loading failures are explicit evidence gaps and do not stop other research. The configured watchlist gives public page/research turns a consistent comparison set, and results retain dated source URLs. Research is still task-directed rather than a continuous crawl and never claims access to competitors' private conversion results.

Measurement excludes URLs carrying the explicit `codex_weekly_diagnostic` query parameter and records the excluded enquiry count separately. Normal attribution parameters remain included. This proposed interpretation of the diagnostic marker requires owner review; it is not a general spam classifier. Untagged tests, spam and traffic without a preserved marker may remain. This change does not rewrite historical imports or the older research-signals dashboard.

## Research dedupe and recovery

The legacy discovery ledger now suppresses only successfully reviewed URL hashes from successful runs for 30 days. Fetch failures, incomplete review, and old ledgers without per-URL completion evidence are eligible for another attempt. Curated prospects remain independently deduplicated regardless of age. Research failure never authorizes repeating an external send.

## Runtime and cutover

1. Review the draft PR and authenticated Vercel Preview, including paused setup, work states, and exact-response approval.
2. Merge through the normal owner-controlled process. There are no schema migrations in this change.
3. Update the dedicated runtime checkout to the reviewed commit. For an existing pilot, preserve its job ID, schedule and owner-approved budgets; do not install a duplicate. For a new installation, `ops/openclaw/install-scout-marketing.sh` creates a disabled weekday hourly wake from 09:00–16:00 America/Toronto with message delivery disabled. This avoids a successful command being reported as failed because no notification destination was configured. It does not enable or modify live schedules automatically. Before any separately approved backlink launch, run `ops/openclaw/preflight-website-growth-backlink-executor.sh`; it validates the installed OAuth route, plugin, skill, tool policy, protected profile and schedule without claiming or sending. During a staggered application/runtime update, legacy unchecked deliveries are saved as waits until the supervisor-capable runtime resumes them.
4. Review the initial services/markets, qualified-enquiry definition, success guidance, competitor watchlist and budgets in Newl Apps. Enable research and the reviewed runtime wake. Keep the independent evidence check-in, approved backlink executor, and build notifier.
5. When the mission is enabled, legacy deep content/backlink preparation returns `managed` and the legacy machine producer returns no draft. Existing approvals and builds remain valid. Disable the redundant legacy deep schedules after confirming the new wake. An in-flight legacy run must finish before cutover.
6. Run one research-only item and review the saved artifact. Run a separately approved controlled outreach test only after reviewing recipient scope. Verify a measurement task against known synthetic preview records.

The worker uses the existing protected Scout environment and subscription-backed Codex resolver. Application credentials remain in the Python wrapper. The model subprocess receives an allowlisted environment, ignores user/project execution rules and user configuration, runs outside a repository in read-only mode, and disables shell tools, apps, plugins, and multi-agent execution. Public search is enabled only for page and open-ended research. No live worker or send is run by unit tests.

Rollback: pause the research mission and disable the new wake. Saved work remains available. Resume legacy schedules only after any new research lease finishes/expires. Never retry an uncertain external message as part of rollback.

## Validation and limitations

Tests cover tenant-bound route authorization, read-only UI, budgets, concurrency rejection, expired/wrong leases, idempotent completion, immediate approval/build projection, stale review rejection, candidate fairness/deduplication, supervisor delivery gates and interruption recovery, partial/missing measurement and deferred windows, independent competitor freshness, draft approval isolation, stale conversation checks, exact-message reservations, and uncertain sends. Existing Website Growth regression suites remain required.

Work history reads remain bounded at 1,000 items and source reconciliation at 60 page candidates, 40 replied opportunities, and 60 published drafts per wake. Worker selection receives at most 50 due items, 20 previous decisions/outcomes and bounded competitive context; full artifacts are loaded only for the claimed item. Google reads still use deployment-configured site/property credentials and capped page reports (25,000 Search Console rows and 10,000 GA4 rows). They are not per-tenant Google account configuration or complete reports for arbitrarily large sites. History archival/pagination, continuous competitor monitoring and CRM qualification/revenue attribution remain future work. The separate website developer workflow, optional model-comparison configuration, and production runtime are not changed automatically. Authentication and vendor outages are surfaced; uncertain sends require human mailbox reconciliation. No SEO or lead-volume outcome is guaranteed.

The initial priority services/markets, qualified-enquiry definition, numerical success guidance and named competitor watchlist are now resolved in the editable mission. Remaining owner decisions are diagnostic-marker treatment, ongoing budget, future changes to the initial profile, and whether future campaign-scoped communication authority should replace individual response approvals. Current code grants no such expanded authority.
